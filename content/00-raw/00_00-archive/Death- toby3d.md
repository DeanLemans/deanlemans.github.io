# Death

I'm thirty now. Hoping to live to at least sixty, I realise that X-Day will happen at any moment and definitely not at the right time. Failure to prepare and leave no instructions behind is a surefire way to cause turmoil and tension among family and friends. I want to prevent that from happening.

This is a living document that will be continually updated. The contents of [https://toby3d.me/en/death/](https://toby3d.me/en/death/) at the time of my death cancels any instructions discussed verbally or in correspondence. I last update it **third February two thousand twenty-fifth year**.

## People

Alyona and Alina must be invited. If they ask to invite any other of our mutual friends and acquaintances – let them. I fully trust their choice as my best and closest friends.

Although I am not officially legally married to Zhibek, I ask that she be treated as my spouse and invited to the ceremony as a member of my family.

Give my friends and my beloved all the help they need if they ask for it. If I can in any way take care of them after my demise at least financially, then do so, because that is what I would do myself.

The same goes for my parents, who raised and nurtured me to be independent and resilient, able to survive even in the darkest of times. I don't hold any grudges, regardless of any disagreements or differences of opinion, because you are my family who somehow always want the best for me.

Sophia and the people associated with her are the only people I don't want at my funeral. They're dead to me. Their contribution to who I have become through betrayal and lies has cost me too much. Please respect my wishes.

Publish the drafts in a separate album with a description and a link about what happened in all platforms, even free ones, as a parting gift to listeners. Don't limit my music to high price and fans in their creativity around mine. Allow my music to be copied and distributed.

### Steam

Valve does not support or approve of third party manipulation of the deceased's library. So you'll have to secretly entrust it to someone so that members of my digital "family" on the Steam platform will still have access to my games.

### Media and messages

Export and archive everything you can reach: through official export tools like Mastodon and Google Takeout, through contacting platform tech support under GDPR, and on your own by direct copying like Yandex.Disk.

I've set up "Google Just In Case" in advance: it will automatically grant access to my Google account to my mum when they records my inactivity for six consecutive months.

Correspondence can be sensitive, so negotiate whether you want access to it at all. Over the years, I've used the following platforms for communications:

- Email: [Yandex.Mail](https://yandex.com/support/mail/download-all-letters.html) and [HEY](https://www.hey.com/faqs/#if-i-don-t-like-hey-can-i-export-my-email-and-contacts)
- [Telegram](https://telegram.org/blog/export-and-more)

Duplicate the data on multiple SSDs: for family, for my closed friends, and for my sweetheart. What to do with that data after – it's up to you.

If you are financially and technically able, I would prefer cremation instead of a traditional burial. Use a reasonable amount of my funds to organise the process.

You may use some particularly good photographs and videos from my archives as a slideshow at the ceremony.
