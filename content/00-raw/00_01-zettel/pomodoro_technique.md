# pomodoro technique

setting a timer for at least 10-20 minutes and in that time, only focus on working. (could be longer or shorter depending on your focus)


after those minutes i can then take a break of 5 (or more) minutes. (kinda depends on how long you want to focus)

usefull as a learning technique
