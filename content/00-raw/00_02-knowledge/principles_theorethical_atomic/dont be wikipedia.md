# dont be wikipedia

In a PKM, it's very easy to get caught in duplicating public knowledge that can be found elsewhere, or is otherwise (pretty well) known.

example:
, a watter bottle, is made up of water, and a container.
There is no benefit in me re-creating that structure here, there is however benefit in tasking/linking/adding original thoughs to a concept?
example:
dont just create note over a book or movie, but also include a small written review about it. just having a note that you almost added nothing to is useless is most cases. with creating a PKM.

recreating public knowledge is inefficient and cost X to manage; time better spent focusing on other shit
