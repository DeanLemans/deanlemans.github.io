# General Substrate Mix

- 1 part, cocos fibre
- 2 part, sphagnum/ground moss(dried)
- 1/4 part, (orchid) bark
- 1/4 part, charcoal(just burned wood, nothing chemical)
- 1/4 part, wormcasting(wormshit)
- springtails(insects)
