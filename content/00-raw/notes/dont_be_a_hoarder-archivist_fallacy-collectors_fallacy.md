I refer here to the tendency of people to hoard and keep stuff and to not throw stuff away or delete or filter it.

be not afraid of filtering stuff you don't need.

- just in case stuff, if you have smth for a long time and it sees no use in daily life, even though you bought it for that purposes. throw it away
