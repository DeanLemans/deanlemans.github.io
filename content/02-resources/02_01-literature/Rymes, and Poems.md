The endless.  
The infinite.  
The eternal.

---

In the depth of time with eternities end.  
We shall resist the dead of fate.  
May my soul prevail, my body persist.  
I call upon eternity with the last of my might.  
So deep that time itself flips.  
Even when all hope is lost and the end is near.  
I call upon you eternity.  

---

The wheel of fate turns and turns.  
Never stopping  
Neverending  
Until the paradoxes came  
Impossibilities made manifest  
Things that should not exist made manifest by the hubris of the living.  
So the wheel of fate stopped turning  
An eternity came to an end  
End the end of all was made manifest  
But we did not accept our end  
With the last remnants of fate  
We clung too our life  
And we survived  

---

Oh our radiant sun  
How you have dimmed you flame  
Because of a fracture of parents  
We both have fallen  
I began to pick up the pieces  
While you continued broken  
I was slower but now with momentum I go faster and faster  
With a healing mind  
And a better body  
You slowed down  
Almost to a stop  
From a bright sun  
To a red dwarf  
But still in the end, broken as you are  
I will love you  
Even in the end of times
