### 👋 Welcome to the Sketch Your Mind Conference

This is a global gathering of visual thinkers who see note-taking as a canvas for creativity, playfulness, collaboration, and storytelling.

During the five days of the conference, you'll hear from world-class leaders in visual thinking, personal knowledge management, design, and more. Expect interactive talks, inspiring ideas, and opportunities to connect with a worldwide community of visual minds. Most sessions will include recordings so you can revisit and reflect afterward.

## About Your Host

Hi, I'm **Zsolt Viczián**, and I'm excited to welcome you to this event. You might know me from the [Visual PKM YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@VisualPKM), the [Excalidraw plugin for Obsidian](https://excalidraw-obsidian.online/), or the [Visual Thinking Workshop](https://visual-thinking-workshop.com/).

This conference is, in many ways, the event I always wished I could attend myself: a gathering of inspiring voices on visual thinking, exploring the topics that fascinate me most. My belief is simple: if I find these conversations meaningful and enjoyable, others will too.

If you'd like to keep exploring beyond the conference, I share ideas and tools for visual knowledge in my book [*Sketch Your Mind*](https://sketch-your-mind.com/).
